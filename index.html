Welcome! Here's how it works

  • Rate the lunch. Tap 1 to 5 biscuits and write a short review. You can change it any time.
  • Then see the results. Everyone's ratings stay hidden until you've rated. Missed lunch? Tap I wasn't in the office to see them anyway, and share what you had instead.
  • Agree or disagree. Use ▲ and ▼ on other people's ratings. The most agreed-with show first.
  • Keep your streak going. Rate every lunch day (Monday to Thursday) for a 🔥. Out-of-office days count too.
  • Check the weekly recap. Lunch of the week, awards, and who shares your taste.
  • Get around. Tap the date to open the calendar, or press ← →.
  • On another laptop or phone? Type the same name there and your ratings come with you.
